A Mutawalli is the trustee of a waqf. The founder decides what the waqf is for; the Mutawalli makes sure that purpose is actually carried out.

## What the role involves

- Looking after the endowed assets and keeping them intact.
- Keeping accurate records of everything received and spent.
- Making sure money reaches the causes the founder chose, and nothing else.
- Reporting back honestly to the founder.

## Why one person should never act alone

Trust is easier to give when it does not depend on one individual being perfect. On Birr, sensitive decisions — approving a distribution, changing an investment, disposing of an asset — need two different people: one proposes, a second approves. The system itself refuses to let the same person do both.

Every such action is also written to a permanent audit trail that cannot be edited afterwards.

## What stays in your hands

You choose the causes and decide how much of your fund each one receives. Birr cannot send more to a cause than you allocated to it.
